How a loaf gets made

Two days, four steps, no shortcuts. This is what happens behind the counter before you walk in.

  1. 1

    Mix & knead

    Stone-ground flour from Mysuru, water, salt and our 6-year-old starter.

  2. 2

    Rest overnight

    Eighteen hours cold. That’s where the flavour and the open crumb come from.

  3. 3

    Bake hot

    Steam-injected deck oven at 250 °C for a crust that crackles as it cools.

  4. 4

    Slice & serve

    Cooled on racks, sliced when you ask, never pre-bagged.
